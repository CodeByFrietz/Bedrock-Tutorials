# 1. Introduction

Welcome to the BaoHost community.

BaoHost provides free Minecraft and Hytale hosting, and maintaining a service that many people can use requires rules, resource limits, security measures, and procedures for handling problems.

Every user is responsible for reading and following the applicable rules.

However, some users ignore the instructions shown on their screen, skip the Terms of Service, or assume that community staff can resolve every problem involving their account.

When something goes wrong, they may immediately ask staff to unlock their account, remove a restriction, bypass a verification requirement, or reverse a suspension without first reading the message explaining what happened.

This creates unnecessary confusion and can delay assistance for other community members.

**Before asking anyone to fix a problem, take the time to understand what the service is telling you.**

Your screen, the official documentation, and the Terms of Service are important sources of information. They may already explain what happened and what you need to do next.

This guide establishes three basic expectations:

1. Read and understand BaoHost's Terms of Service before using the service.
2. Read the entire error message or warning displayed on your screen before taking action.
3. Understand that community staff have limited responsibilities and cannot override official account-security or suspension decisions.

These expectations apply to everyone, including new users and experienced server owners.

# 2. Why Reading Before Acting Matters

Reading the instructions before doing something is not an unnecessary step. It is part of using a service responsibly.

When you use BaoHost, you may encounter:

- Account registration instructions.
- Login errors.
- Verification requirements.
- Security warnings.
- Restricted-access pages.
- Suspension notices.
- Server installation instructions.
- File-upload limitations.
- Resource limits.
- Community rules.
- Terms of Service updates.

These messages are displayed because they contain information relevant to the action you are performing.

Ignoring them can cause confusion, incorrect assumptions, unnecessary support requests, or further violations of the rules.

## 2.1. Read the message before asking for help

For example, imagine you try to access your account and the page displays:

```text
Your account has been suspended.

Please review the information provided and submit an appeal if available.
```

The appropriate response is not to immediately create another account or ask community staff to bypass the restriction.

Instead:

1. Read the entire message.
2. Identify whether the page provides a suspension explanation.
3. Check your email for additional information, if applicable.
4. Follow the official instructions.
5. Use the appeal procedure if it is available and appropriate.

The message is the starting point for understanding what happened.

## 2.2. Do not click through warnings without understanding them

A warning may explain that an action could affect your account, delete information, or change your access.

Before confirming an action, ask yourself:

- What exactly am I about to do?
- Why is this warning being displayed?
- Could this affect my account or server?
- Is there a safer way to proceed?
- Do I understand the consequences?

If you do not understand the warning, stop and look for the appropriate documentation.

## 2.3. Do not assume that every problem is a technical bug

A user may assume that they cannot access an account because BaoHost is broken.

However, possible explanations include:

- A temporary browser or network issue.
- An incorrect login credential.
- An unfinished verification process.
- A security challenge.
- A restriction related to the connection being used.
- An account suspension.
- A violation of the Terms of Service.

These situations require different responses.

The correct next step depends on the actual message and the circumstances.

## 2.4. Reading instructions helps everyone

When users read the documentation and follow the correct procedure, they can often solve straightforward problems without needing someone else to explain every step.

This also allows community staff to focus on questions that genuinely require their attention.

**The expectation is simple: read first, understand the situation, follow the instructions, and ask for help when you still need it.**

# 3. Understanding the BaoHost Terms of Service

The Terms of Service, commonly abbreviated as **TOS**, describe the rules and conditions that apply when you use BaoHost.

You can read the official document here:

**https://baohost.com/terms**

As of the date of this guide, BaoHost's published Terms of Service have an effective date of January 26, 2026.

The TOS explains important responsibilities and restrictions involving:

- Account eligibility.
- Account security.
- The number of accounts and servers permitted.
- VPN and proxy usage.
- Acceptable use of hosting resources.
- Prohibited content and activities.
- Account and server suspension.
- Service availability.
- Inactivity and deletion.

BaoHost's published TOS states that users must be at least 13 years old, or meet the minimum age required in their country.

It also states that users are responsible for maintaining the security of their account and password.

The terms prohibit account sharing.

This means you should keep your login credentials private and should not give another person access to your account.

Do not share your password with friends, strangers, community moderators, or people claiming they can fix an account restriction.

Legitimate staff should not need your password to explain the public rules or direct you to the correct support procedure.

## 3.3. Acceptable use

BaoHost's TOS prohibits certain types of content, resource abuse, and activities that can harm the service or other users.

Examples include:

- Crypto-mining.
- DDoS attacks.
- Hosting unrelated web servers.
- Using bots or scripts to bypass inactivity restrictions.
- Uploading prohibited or illegal content.
- Installing malicious plugins or mods designed to harm the host node or gain unauthorized access.
- Operating a prohibited multi-server network using multiple free servers.

These restrictions exist because BaoHost must manage shared resources and protect its infrastructure.

A free hosting service cannot safely allow every user to use unlimited resources or perform unrestricted activities.

## 3.4. Service availability and backups

BaoHost's TOS states that it does not guarantee 100% uptime and that users are responsible for their own backups.

The terms also state that servers may be suspended or deleted after more than 30 days of inactivity, subject to the current dashboard requirements.

This means you should not assume that your world or server files will always remain available without maintenance.

Make regular backups and follow the activity requirements shown by the service.

## 3.5. Consequences of violating the rules

Depending on the circumstances and applicable policy, violations may lead to warnings, restrictions, suspension, or termination.

Do not assume that a rule does not apply simply because you did not read it.

If a restriction appears, read the explanation and follow the official procedure.

# 4. The One-Account and One-Server Rule

One of the most important rules in BaoHost's TOS concerns accounts and servers.

The published terms state:

> One person may only have one account and one server.

The TOS also states that multi-accounting is strictly prohibited and will result in a ban.

Users should read the exact wording in the current policy:

https://baohost.com/terms

## 4.1. What does this mean?

Do not create or use multiple BaoHost accounts to obtain additional free servers, bypass restrictions, or work around a limit applied to your original account.

For example, these are not acceptable ways to work around a restriction:

- Creating another account because your original account was suspended.
- Registering several accounts to obtain multiple free servers.
- Creating another account to bypass a server or account limit.
- Using another person's account to continue access that you lost.
- Asking someone else to create an account for you as a way to bypass a restriction.

The current TOS also prohibits account sharing.

## 4.2. What if you forgot your login information?

Forgetting your password does not mean you should automatically create another account.

First, use the available official account-recovery process.

If you cannot recover access, follow the official instructions for contacting BaoHost.

Do not create another account as a substitute for resolving the original account problem.

## 4.3. What if you already have more than one account?

Do not create any additional accounts.

Read the current TOS and contact the appropriate official support channel for guidance about your situation.

Do not assume that creating more accounts will resolve the problem.

## 4.4. Why is this rule enforced?

BaoHost provides shared hosting resources.

Creating multiple accounts can allow a single person to occupy more resources than permitted, bypass restrictions, or evade previous enforcement actions.

This can reduce available resources for other users.

BaoHost explains this issue in its official article:

https://www.baohost.com/blog/vpns-proxies-and-alt-accounts

**Remember: Losing access to one account does not give you permission to create another account to bypass the restriction.**

# 5. VPNs, Proxies, and Restricted Access

VPN and proxy usage is another important part of BaoHost's access rules.

The current TOS states that the use of VPN or proxy services is prohibited and may result in a ban.

BaoHost's Restricted Access Error guide also instructs users to turn off VPNs when accessing the website.

Official references:

- TOS: https://baohost.com/terms
- Restricted Access Error (Code 567): https://help.baohost.com/articles/website-blocked
- VPNs, Proxies, and Alt Accounts: https://www.baohost.com/blog/vpns-proxies-and-alt-accounts

## 5.1. What is a VPN?

A VPN, or Virtual Private Network, routes your internet traffic through another network.

A proxy can also change how your connection appears to a website.

These technologies have legitimate uses in many situations. However, BaoHost's published rules restrict VPN and proxy use for its service.

Always follow BaoHost's current policy when accessing the service.

## 5.2. Why might a VPN or proxy cause an access problem?

BaoHost explains that its security systems may check sign-ups and logins against known VPN, proxy, Tor, and high-risk network addresses.

A connection that triggers a security check may be restricted or reviewed.

Possible contributing circumstances include:

- Accessing BaoHost through a prohibited VPN.
- Using a proxy service.
- Connecting through an IP address associated with suspicious activity.
- Attempting to create multiple accounts.
- Patterns of access that trigger automated anti-abuse checks.

This does not mean every restricted-access message proves that the user intentionally violated a rule.

BaoHost's own Restricted Access guide states that it does not have specific information explaining why every individual or region encounters a Tencent or Cloudflare block page.

Do not assume you know the precise cause without reading the displayed message and the applicable account information.

## 5.3. What should you do if you see Restricted Access Error (Code 567)?

BaoHost provides the following general troubleshooting steps:

1. Read the entire message displayed on the page.
2. Try another browser or a private/incognito window.
3. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
4. Turn off any VPN or proxy.
5. Try a normal internet connection, such as your regular Wi-Fi or mobile data.
6. If a Cloudflare challenge appears, allow it to finish or follow the on-screen instructions.
7. If the restriction remains, consult the official Help Center.

Official guide:

https://help.baohost.com/articles/website-blocked

**Important:** These steps apply to a website-access or security challenge. They are not instructions for bypassing an account suspension.

If the page specifically states that your account is suspended, follow the suspension procedure instead.

## 5.4. What should you NOT do?

If access is restricted, do not:

- Create multiple accounts to test whether one works.
- Use another person's account.
- Continue using a VPN or proxy contrary to the TOS.
- Attempt to evade a suspension.
- Repeatedly submit requests without reading the message.
- Demand that community staff remove the restriction.

Follow the official instructions instead.

# 7. What to Do When You Cannot Access Your Account

If you cannot access your BaoHost account, do not immediately create another account or assume that the system is broken.

First, determine what type of problem you are experiencing.

Different problems require different actions.

## 7.1. Incorrect password

If the page says your password is incorrect:

1. Check that you entered the correct account information.
2. Check for accidental spaces or typing errors.
3. Use the official password-recovery option if one is available.
4. Follow the instructions displayed by the service.

Do not share your password with community staff or other users.

## 7.2. Email verification or another required step

If the page tells you to verify an email address or complete another required step, read the instructions carefully.

Complete the requested step through the official interface.

If you cannot complete it, use the support channel identified by the service.

## 7.3. Restricted Access Error (Code 567)

This is a particular type of access restriction that may display a Tencent or Cloudflare block page.

BaoHost explains that the exact reason may not be known for every case.

Follow the official guide:

https://help.baohost.com/articles/website-blocked

A Code 567 page does not automatically tell a community member whether your account has been suspended.

Read the entire message and distinguish a website-access challenge from an explicit suspension notice.

## 7.4. Account suspension page

If the page tells you that your account has been suspended, follow the suspension instructions.

BaoHost states that suspension notices may include an explanation and that an email may be sent where possible.

If an appeal option is available, use it.

Do not try to resolve a suspension by creating an alternate account or using someone else's account.

## 7.5. Website or service appears unavailable

If you receive a general website error rather than an account-specific notice:

1. Read the full error message.
2. Check the official Help Center.
3. Check for relevant service announcements.
4. Try again later if the service reports an outage.
5. Contact official support if the issue persists.

Do not assume a temporary website error proves that your account has been banned.

## 7.6. You still do not understand the message

If the error remains unclear:

1. Copy the exact wording.
2. Take a screenshot if appropriate.
3. Remove private information from the screenshot before sharing it.
4. Read the relevant help article.
5. Determine whether the problem belongs to account recovery, restricted access, suspension, or a general technical issue.
6. Use the corresponding official channel.

The goal is to identify the category of the problem before choosing a solution.

# 8. Understanding Error Messages and Warnings

One of the most avoidable problems in online communities is asking for assistance without reading the information already provided.

When the panel displays an error, do not ignore it.

Read the message from beginning to end.

## 8.1. Look for specific instructions

An on-screen message may explain:

- What happened.
- Why an action could not proceed.
- Whether additional verification is needed.
- Whether a restriction is active.
- Whether you need to contact official support.
- Whether an appeal is available.
- What you should do next.

Following those instructions is the appropriate starting point.

## 8.2. Do not assume every message means the same thing

For example:

| Situation | What it may mean | Correct next step |
|---|---|---|
| Incorrect password | The credentials were not accepted | Use the official recovery process |
| Verification required | An account step has not been completed | Follow the verification instructions |
| Code 567 | Website access is being restricted or challenged | Follow the Restricted Access guide |
| Explicit suspension notice | The account has been suspended | Read the suspension notice and use the appeal procedure if available |
| Website unavailable | A temporary or broader service issue may exist | Check official notices and the Help Center |
| Server panel error | A server or hosting operation may have failed | Read the error and relevant technical documentation |

These examples are not interchangeable.

A community member should not guess that someone is suspended just because they cannot load the website, nor should an explicit suspension message be treated as an ordinary browser problem.

## 8.3. Read the full message before posting

When asking for help, include the actual error message rather than saying only:

> It does not work.

A clear description makes it easier to direct you toward the correct procedure.

However, community staff are not required to diagnose private account restrictions or override official decisions.

They can point you to the available documentation and support process when appropriate.

# 9. What to Do If Your Account Is Suspended

If BaoHost displays a suspension page, read it carefully.

Do not ignore it, close it without understanding it, or immediately attempt to register another account.

BaoHost's official policy explains the process.

Official article:

https://help.baohost.com/articles/suspended

## Step 1: Read the suspension notice

Check the information displayed on the suspended account page.

The page may explain that the account has been suspended and direct you to the appeal process.

## Step 2: Check your email

BaoHost states that it may send an email with more information where possible.

Check the email account associated with your BaoHost account.

## Step 3: Review the relevant rules

Read the TOS and consider whether your recent activity may have violated a rule.

Do not simply assume that the suspension is a mistake without reviewing the information available to you.

Equally, do not assume that you know the exact reason unless the official notice or support process establishes it.

## Step 4: Submit an appeal if one is available

If the suspension page provides a **Submit Appeal** button:

1. Open the appeal form.
2. Explain your situation clearly.
3. State what you were doing before the restriction occurred.
4. Address the relevant information in the notice.
5. Be honest and provide relevant details.
6. Submit the appeal.

Do not submit knowingly false information.

## Step 5: Wait for the official review

The appeal process is handled through BaoHost's designated process.

Community staff cannot substitute for that process or guarantee a particular outcome.

Do not repeatedly contact unrelated community members demanding that they reverse the suspension.

## Step 6: If the appeal is denied

BaoHost's policy states that another appeal may be available from the same suspension page when the option remains available.

If it is available and you have relevant information, follow the instructions there.

If a final decision has been issued, the official policy states that no further appeals are available for that account.

# 10. What Is a Final Suspension Decision?

A final suspension decision is different from an ordinary login problem or a temporary technical error.

BaoHost's Suspended Accounts guide explains that, in cases where a final decision email is issued:

- The account is permanently suspended.
- The user can no longer submit new appeals for that account.
- The service cannot be provided under that account.
- Support agents cannot override, bypass, or reopen the suspension.

Official source:

https://help.baohost.com/articles/suspended

## What does this mean for users?

If the official notice says a final decision has been issued, do not expect community staff to reverse it.

Do not create additional accounts in an attempt to continue using the service.

The published policy specifically warns that creating new accounts after a permanent suspension may result in the new accounts being terminated.

## Why can community staff not override it?

Community staff do not necessarily have the administrative permissions required to change account status.

More importantly, the official suspension policy states that a final decision cannot be overridden or bypassed by support agents.

This is a policy limitation, not an invitation to seek another staff member who might make an exception.

**If a final decision has been issued, respect the official outcome.**

- Private account records.
- Authentication systems.

## Important clarification

Community staff may point you to the correct procedure, but they should not be treated as a replacement for official account support.

If an issue requires account verification, investigation, or an appeal decision, follow the official process.

**Being a community staff member does not automatically grant permission to unlock accounts, remove suspensions, access private records, or override official decisions.**

If the account page provides instructions, follow them.

Community staff may direct you toward those resources, but they cannot promise to perform an action they are not authorized to perform.

# 14. The Difference Between Community Support and Official Support

Understanding this distinction helps prevent unnecessary arguments and delays.

## Community support

Community support generally takes place in community spaces, such as discussion channels.

It may involve general questions, publicly available technical guidance, and links to relevant documentation.

Community members and staff may be able to help you understand a problem, but they may not have access to private account records or administrative controls.

## Official support

Official support handles issues through the channels designated by BaoHost.

Depending on the issue, the official process may involve account-related inquiries, technical problems, or appeals.

Account suspensions should follow the process described in the official Suspended Accounts guide.

## Which channel should you use?

| Problem | Appropriate starting point |
|---|---|
| General Minecraft server question | Community documentation or community support |
| Question about a public BaoHost guide | Read the guide, then ask for clarification if needed |
| Forgotten password | Official account-recovery process, if available |
| Explicit suspension notice | Suspended account page and appeal instructions, if available |
| Final suspension decision | Respect the final decision; do not seek a bypass |
| Restricted Access Error (Code 567) | Official Restricted Access guide |
| Website outage | Official notices and Help Center |
| Question about what the TOS means | Read the TOS and contact the official support channel for clarification if necessary |
| Server crashes or configuration problems | Technical troubleshooting guide, logs, and the appropriate technical support channel |

Community staff are not required to take ownership of a private account case simply because it is posted in a community channel.

# 15. How to Ask for Help Properly

When a problem occurs, provide enough information to understand what you are experiencing.

A useful request should explain the issue without demanding that someone bypass a rule.

## Before asking

1. Read the complete error message.
2. Check the relevant official documentation.
3. Follow the instructions already provided.
4. Identify whether the issue involves your login, a website restriction, or an account suspension.
5. Record what you have already tried.
6. Ask the appropriate support channel if the issue remains unresolved.

## Example of an unhelpful request

> I can't get into my account. Someone fix it.

This gives very little information and does not explain what the screen displays.

## Example of a better request

> I cannot access the BaoHost panel. The page displays a restricted-access message. I have read the official guide and followed the applicable troubleshooting steps, but the issue remains. I would like to know which official procedure applies to my situation.

This request describes the problem and shows that the user has made a reasonable effort to follow the instructions.

If the page explicitly says that the account is suspended, use the suspension appeal process instead of treating the issue as an ordinary website-access error.

## Protect your privacy
